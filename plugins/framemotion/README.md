# FrameMotion

Motion graphics from code: from idea and storyboard to a rendered MP4 with frame-by-frame QA and designed sound when wanted. Kinetic type, animated titles and logos, explainers, app and product films from screenshots or photos, data animation, built with HyperFrames, canvas, GSAP or Remotion.

FrameMotion works on its own, with its own short brief, and as the motion companion of [FrameCore Works Creative Studio](https://github.com/FrameCoreWorks/framecore-works-creative-studio). With Studio, Studio keeps the brief, idea, copy locks, board approval and delivery; FrameMotion does the motion plan, build, render and frame QA.

- One skill: [`skills/framemotion/SKILL.md`](skills/framemotion/SKILL.md).
- Motion workshop and decision table: [`references/workshop/`](skills/framemotion/references/workshop/README.md).
- Remotion route: [`remotion/REMOTION.md`](skills/framemotion/remotion/REMOTION.md).
- Shared task contract with Studio, Toolbox and Open Assets: [`contracts/framecore-task.schema.json`](contracts/framecore-task.schema.json).

Requirements depend on the host: without code execution FrameMotion writes the storyboard and the motion contract for the motion player; with Python, Pillow and FFmpeg it renders through the Python renderer; with a shell, Node.js, FFmpeg and Chrome or Chromium it builds HyperFrames, canvas, GSAP and Remotion compositions. No account or API key is needed. Remotion is paid for companies above three people; FrameMotion warns before choosing it.

In Claude Code: `/plugin install framemotion --marketplace FrameCoreWorks/framemotion`. Other hosts: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/FrameCoreWorks/framemotion/blob/main/INSTALL.md).
