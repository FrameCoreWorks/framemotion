## 0.5.0 (2026-11-19)

- **Reel workflow, version 2** (owner messages 2026-11-19: the stages of a reel and the quality loop). `references/reel-workflow.md` is rewritten as the one source of truth: work as a motion designer, a film editor and a composer at once; five stages, four of them shown for acceptance: (1) idea and look in one package (hook, firm scene times, two or three finished key frames, music direction from the user's music profile, one reference asked for; two or three concepts with a named wow for an ad without an idea, exactly one for a precise brief), (2) the music as an MP3 composed to the plan's times (-14 LUFS, true peak below -1 dBTP, original or CC0, real instrument timbres, effects only sketched), (3) a contact sheet on the music's beats whose acceptance is the picture lock, (4) the 540p preview with full effects and the final mix (music may change its sound on the same grid; a new grid goes back to stage 2), (5) the 1080 render only on explicit request. Correction rules: notes collected per stage, at most two user rounds, a third means back to stage 1, notes become rule corrections in the user's own files and the music profile, process changes from the next project. The quality loop before every stage: each profession's checklist (frame strips at 4 to 5 fps with automatic checks on every frame and close-ups at text and cuts; cuts on the beat; loudness, grid, spectrum and a mono fold-down by measurement), then a fresh reviewer with only the brief, frames and measurements (score below 8 means fix), at most three passes, and one sentence on what the loop fixed and the weakest spot. New `templates/music-profile.md`. Sound sources follow the owner's rulings of the same day in the shared compendium: music is a CC0 recording with real instruments when one fits, otherwise composed in code and played by real CC0 instruments, never a Content ID library, and the stage 2 MP3 is the listening sample before the preview; effects for real objects are recordings of that object, code only for abstract accents. `motion-sound-design.md` (timing first instead of picture first), `layered-work.md` (layers mapped to the new stages) and `SKILL.md` follow it.
- **Real instruments for composed music.** New `assets/motion-sound/samples.py` and `samples-vsco2ce.json`: the composer's parts can be played by recorded instruments from VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (CC0-1.0, pinned commit 4403009): upright piano, harp, marimba, glockenspiel, flute, violin section, cello section and double-bass pizzicato. Set with `sound.py plan --set lead=real-piano --set pad=real-strings --set bass=real-pizz-bass` (`pad` and `bass` are new roles for `--set`). Nothing is bundled: `sound.py mix` fetches only the instruments the recipe uses into `~/.cache/framemotion/samples` (or `$FRAMEMOTION_SAMPLES`), checks size and git blob hash; offline, the same notes are synthesized and the mix says so. Several source folders name files an octave below the sounding pitch; the manifest stores the measured pitch. New `tests/motion_samples_test.py` and `tests/reel_workflow_test.py`.
- Evidence: licence read from the repository (GitHub licence field CC0-1.0, README credits) on 2026-11-19; piano, strings and pizz-bass fetched on the Mac (51 MB) and `samples.py verify` measured the sounding pitch of every zone in the measurable range within 0.12 semitone (one low violin note reads its second harmonic; its spectrum shows the 220 Hz series, so the zone is right); a 12 s test bed rendered with real piano, strings and bass. Nobody has listened to it yet (`NOT_RUN`). No reel was rendered. Codex and ChatGPT Work `NOT_TESTED (by policy)`.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

First release of FrameMotion as a complete standalone plugin, which Creative Studio can also use as its motion companion.

- One skill, `framemotion`, with all references, templates, starters and engines (Animate, Anatomy, motion toolkit, motion review, render, sound, sync, variants and the rest), and a Remotion route under `skills/framemotion/remotion/`.
- Standalone brief, mode rules (standalone or Studio companion) and the motion workshop with its decision table, written as general defaults.
- Bundled fonts (OFL) and its own hashed copy of `captions.py`, so FrameMotion needs no other plugin.
- Shared `framecore-task-result/1` contract schema, byte-identical with Studio, Toolbox and Open Assets.
- Tests: 22 motion test files plus repository checks for manifests, links, the contract hash and the captions copy.
- Links to Studio files read "Creative Studio: `path`" and apply only when Studio is installed.
