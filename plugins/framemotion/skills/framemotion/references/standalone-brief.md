# Standalone brief

Use this when FrameMotion runs without Creative Studio, or when Studio sent no brief. When Studio sent a task envelope, its brief, idea, copy locks and board approval are the inputs and this file is skipped.

Ask only for what is missing, one question at a time, and stop asking as soon as the film can be planned. Reuse anything the user already gave.

1. **Purpose and audience.** What the film must make the viewer know, feel or do, and who watches it.
2. **Format and length.** Aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9 or several), length in seconds, platform. When the requested length cannot hold the copy at readable holds (the [reading clock](workshop/typography.md)), propose the shortest length that fits before building.
3. **Exact copy.** Every word that must appear on screen, verbatim, with language and spelling locked. Nothing is invented for on-screen text without the user's approval.
4. **Brand and assets.** Logo, colours, fonts, photos, screenshots or footage, and the right to use them. No brand supplied: propose a neutral look derived from the idea, never a house style.
5. **Sound.** None, music bed, designed effects, voice-over or a supplied track.

Then write the brief as five short lines (purpose, format, copy, look, sound), mark each item Confirmed, Proposed or Unknown, and continue with step 2 of the method in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md): two or three different concepts with a named "wow" moment for an open brief, one storyboard for an approved concept.

Ownership without Studio: FrameMotion keeps the brief, the idea, the copy locks, the board approval and the delivery itself. Links in this skill marked "Creative Studio: `...`" point to Studio files that exist only when Studio is installed; without it, follow the rule written next to the link and skip the file.
