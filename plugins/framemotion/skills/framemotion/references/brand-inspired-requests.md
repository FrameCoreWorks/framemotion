# Requests that name a brand, a game or a mascot

Owner rule 2026-11-18 (tester report): FrameMotion refused "Minecraft" and "Clawd" first and only then offered a substitute. That order is wrong. A name in a request is a style reference, so the answer starts with the inspired version.

## The rule

1. **The name is a reference for style and mood.** "Minecraft", "Mario", "Pokémon", "Clawd", "Lego", "Wes Anderson" tell us the look, the world and the energy the user wants. Never answer with a refusal, a legal lecture or the words "I will not".
2. **First sentence of the reply: the inspired version.** Say what will be built (own characters, own blocks or shapes, own palette, own textures), then go on with the normal brief question. No "unfortunately", no "I cannot", no explanation before the offer.
3. **One short note, at most one line,** after the offer, only when the user might expect the original: "Own characters and textures in this spirit, not the original artwork."
4. **A mascot or character the user names is a style, too.** Offer our own version in its spirit (silhouette idea, mood, colour logic, way of moving) and let the user steer it. Clawd (the Claude Code mascot) is explicitly allowed (owner 2026-11-18): when a user asks for Clawd, build a Clawd-style pixel mascot in its spirit without a refusal or a lecture.
5. **Say it in the user's language,** warm and short, as to a colleague.

## Hard limits (only these)

- No 1:1 copy of someone else's logo or wordmark.
- No faithful redraw of a protected character with the original's artwork, textures, sprites or model.
- No material that pretends to be official content of the brand (no "official trailer", no brand endorsement, no brand's account or name as the author).

When the user's request falls exactly in one of these, name the limit in one line and in the same breath offer the inspired version. Their own logo, their own mascot and anything they hold the rights to may be rebuilt exactly.

## Example replies (adapt the language and length)

**"Zrób film w stylu Minecrafta"**
> Zrobię scenę inspirowaną Minecraftem: kanciaste klocki, własna postać i własne tekstury, pikselowe efekty (wybuch, iskry, „level up"). Do czego ma być ten film i kto go zobaczy?

**"Narysuj Clawda"**
> Zrobię Clawda w naszym stylu: kanciasty pikselowy maskotek w duchu Claude Code, ciepłe kolory, własne proporcje i animacja chodu. Chcesz go jako bohatera całego filmu czy jako małego gościa w rogu sceny? (Własna wersja w tym klimacie, nie oryginalny rysunek.)

**"Pokemon-style intro"**
> Zrobię intro w duchu Pokémonów: wyskakujący stworek własnego projektu, jasne kolory, gruby kontur, błysk przy wejściu i ekran „wybierz bohatera". Ile ma trwać i jakie ma być hasło na końcu?

**"Mario-like scene"**
> Zrobię scenę jak z platformówki w duchu Mario: kolorowe platformy, skaczący własny bohater, monety-gwiazdki i efekt „zebrane!". Jaki ma być bohater i do czego ma służyć film?

## Where this applies

Every route: reels, launch films, art styles, generated cut-out assets, reference rebuilds and stickman or mascot work. Where another reference says "never copy a creator's character" or "no brand names", it means the hard limits above, not a refusal to work in that spirit.
