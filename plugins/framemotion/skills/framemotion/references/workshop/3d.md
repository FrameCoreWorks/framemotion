# 3D and Blender

When 3D is right: [decision table](decisions.md).

**When Blender is worth it.** A film built on a physical material in close-up (paper, fabric, glass, liquid, food, a product), contact shadows, translucency or macro depth of field: realism that three.js on a software renderer does not give. Use it for hero shots and big moments, combined in FFmpeg with 2D and kinetic type made in code; text and the end card stay in code. Not worth it for typographic, UI or flat 2D films, dense multi-scene edits, a same-day deadline without a capable machine, or realistic hands and characters.

**Measured cost.** Four CPU cores without a GPU: 720×1280, 32 samples plus OIDN denoise, about 40 s per frame, so a 30-second 1080p film takes many hours. Render only control frames there (540×960, 24 samples, about 19 s per frame, a sheet of three or four frames); render the full film on a machine with a GPU (Apple Metal or CUDA) and measure its speed on the first render.

**Pipeline.** Everything procedural in one script (geometry, materials, light, camera, per-frame animation through vertices); `bpy` works as a Python module where no Blender binary exists. Sound from recordings or synthesised accents, muxed with FFmpeg.

**Fixing "cheap 3D".** Always tone mapping (AgX, the default since Blender 4.0; Khronos PBR Neutral for products with faithful colour, since 4.2). Area lights at least the size of the object, not point lights (a soft shadow terminator). HDRI for reflections, a studio one for products. Different temperatures for key and rim. Brightness through exposure, not by pushing lamps. A bevel on every edge and Smooth by Angle (since 4.1). Applied object scale and real-world dimensions, because depth of field, subsurface scattering and light falloff depend on them. A contact shadow or AO. Focus on the leading edge of the object. A little aberration, grain and vignette at the end.

**PBR rules.** Base colour never pure white or black. Metallic only 0 or 1 (rust against metal is a mask, not 0.5). Dielectrics reflect about 4% head-on (F0 0.04); Fresnel comes from the IOR: water 1.33, glass about 1.5, plastic about 1.45-1.5, diamond 2.42. Roughness is never perfectly even: an imperfection map (scratches, smudges) as Non-Color. Lacquer is clearcoat, fabric is sheen, brushed metal is anisotropy. Organic matter (skin, meat, cheese, leaves, wax) needs subsurface scattering or it looks like clay.

**Food.** Subsurface scattering at world scale. Freshness and glisten: clearcoat with fine noise in Clearcoat Normal. Toppings through Geometry Nodes (Distribute Points on Faces plus Instance on Points) with density painted by weights. A pour of cheese or sauce is sculpted, not simulated. Steam and smoke as real recordings in Screen/Add instead of volumetrics. Light mostly from behind (the scattering glows) with a large soft source as a window for highlights.

**Light and camera.** Key in front and to the side, higher; fill on the other side, much weaker; a narrow, strong rim behind to separate from the background. Tech and cosmetics: narrow strips of light at steep angles on the edges. Moody: almost no HDRI, soft-edged spots. Product hero on a long lens (85-135 mm); wide only for POV and space; spaces at 35-50 mm. Camera moves like a heavy dolly or crane: a slow push, a small range. Shutter 180°.

**Post order.** Depth of field, motion blur, bloom on HDR values, tone mapping, correction or LUT, anti-aliasing.

**3D letters and logos.** Bevel, merge duplicate vertices (By Distance), Smooth by Angle, front faces as quads for close-ups (n-gons spoil reflections). Chrome or glass with a strong rim reads better than matte. Offset letter layers slightly (z-fighting).

**Passes for compositing in code.** Render with a transparent background (Film > Transparent): beauty, shadow catcher, AO, Z-depth. 2D motion, text and the end card sit in code above and below these layers.

**Paper, a tested example.** Real scale (a sheet 0.15 m); light power for a small scene is small (an area light around 14 W; 220 W burned out the picture). Solidify 0.1-0.2 mm with a white underside, Principled with translucency about 0.2, sheen 0.35, a fibre noise bump, AgX Medium High Contrast. A large table plane (6 m) with wood from noise, not a regular weave (moiré). Lights with glossy visibility off, or a bright patch stays on the table. Camera 50 mm at f/5.6. A fold animates by rolling around a radius that shrinks as it presses down, with a slight spring-back (about 0.09 rad); lift from a corner.

**three.js renders.** A `seek(t)` engine with Playwright is deterministic; motion blur by averaging sub-frames (FFmpeg `tmix`). Colour: `renderer.outputColorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace`, colour textures in sRGB and data maps (normal, roughness, metallic) linear. Anti-alias by rendering at 2× and scaling down.
